# Formatter icon

Version 0.1.6 uses a smaller Revo R with small `fmt` text to its right. The
source was the user-supplied Revo icon previously shipped in 0.1.5.

The visual edit used the built-in image generation tool. ImageMagick resized
the selected output to 512 × 512, filled unintended interior transparency with
charcoal, and restored the original outer-corner alpha mask. The packaged asset
is `images/icon.png`.

Preserve every part of the tile as solid opaque dark charcoal, including the area inside the orange circle. Only the tiny corner spaces OUTSIDE the rounded tile are transparent. Keep the original perfectly flat solid fills, original thick orange ring and original rounded square shape. Inside the orange ring, make the original off-white stylized R smaller, about two thirds its original height, position it left of center, and put small lowercase "fmt" immediately to its right. Use bold rounded off-white letters, letter height about one third of the smaller R height, centered vertically. Preserve the unique four-stroke R silhouette. Center the R plus fmt group as a whole. Every dark charcoal pixel on the tile must be FULLY OPAQUE. No holes, speckles, texture, lighting, shadows or gradients anywhere; this is a clean flat app icon. Preserve solid dark material across the entire tile. Square PNG.
